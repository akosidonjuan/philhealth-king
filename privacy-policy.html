Privacy policy

PHILHEALTH KING · last updated 11 November 2026

PHILHEALTH KING is a personal lookup and log app for PhilHealth case rates, developed by Dr. Juan Carlo Navarrete. It is made for the doctor who uses it. The developer does not collect, receive, or see your data.

What is stored on your device

Your case log, places, favourites, doctor choice, theme, and optional PIN stay on the phone or browser you are using (in the app’s local storage). Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to sign in or you export a backup file yourself.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google, the app copies your log, places and favourites to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive (the Drive appDataFolder). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. There is no PHILHEALTH KING server. The copy is used only to keep your own devices in step. Sign out at any time; the data on the device you are using stays there.

Sign-in is currently in Google’s testing mode: Google may ask you to sign in again about every 7 days.

What the developer does not collect

There is no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or account on a PHILHEALTH KING server. Patient names in your log are for your personal records only and are never shared or made public.

Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

This app is designed so that you remain the one holding the records. Names and case details stay on your device, and in your own Google Drive app folder if you turn on sync. They are not transferred to the developer.

Backups you export yourself

A JSON backup you save or share is a complete copy of your log, including full names. Store it somewhere private.
